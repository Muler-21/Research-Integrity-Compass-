Research Integrity Compass (RIC)የምርምር ታማኝነት ኮምፓስ

See every paper your university publishes, measured against international standards and your own rules.

RIC gathers your institution's publications from OpenAlex, checks every journal for quality and integrity, ranks publishers, and screens publication-reward claims. It runs in your browser and is free to use.

  • Free for everyone, with no sign-up
  • Data from OpenAlex, the open catalogue of the world's research
  • Indicators follow DORA and the Leiden Manifesto
  • Journal levels from Finland's JUFO and Norway's register

OpenAlex access

Optional. RIC works without a key: OpenAlex gives every visitor a small free daily allowance and up to 10,000 results per search. Heavy users can add a free key from openalex.org/settings/api for ten times the allowance and no 10,000 limit. A key is saved only in this browser.

International channel levels (optional, recommended)

Official journal levels used by Nordic universities and funders — 2 = leading, 1 = approved, 0 = not approved. Export the CSV from the JUFO portal or the Norwegian Register and upload it; journals are matched by ISSN.

Use the results responsibly

Tiers and levels are signals built from OpenAlex data and the lists you load. A flag is not proof that a journal is predatory, and publishing in one is not misconduct: authors are often deceived, and journals get hijacked or cloned. Check each case before acting, and don't publish lists of named researchers on this basis alone.
